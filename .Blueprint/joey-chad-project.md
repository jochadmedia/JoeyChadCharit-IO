**Project Mission:** To keep Joey Chad's memory alive through football by creating digital products that inspire, teach, and connect fans worldwide, with all proceeds supporting Joey's charity.

**Key Products:**
1.  **Joey AI Football Coach (MVP):** AI-powered personal coach using Joey's content for skill training.
2.  **SkillSwap: Learn from Legends:** Social learning with AI video analysis for side-by-side skill comparison.
3.  **The Joey Challenge:** Gamified skill mastery with charity leaderboards.
4.  **Joey's Football Memory Lane (AR App):** Augmented reality experience showcasing Joey's journey.
5.  **Joey Football Academy:** Subscription-based structured football education.

**Why:** Joey Chad was a passionate footballer who passed away tragically before a professional trial. This project honors his legacy and passion, creating a positive impact through charity.

**How to apply:** When assisting with tasks related to this project, prioritize decisions that align with the mission of honoring Joey's memory, supporting his charity, and developing the five core product ideas. Refer to the `JOeney-Chad-Project-Roadmap.md` for detailed timelines, technical stack, and business model. The primary focus for immediate development is the "Joey AI Football Coach" MVP.
